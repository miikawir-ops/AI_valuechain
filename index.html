What is Market Regime?

A systemic filter applied to all layer scores. Reflects the overall macro environment for AI investments today.

Risk-On — no risk signal and at most one neutral one. Scores at full strength.
Neutral — some macro stress. Scores dampened.
Risk-Off — broad macro stress. Scores dampened strongly.

Signals used: VIX · 10Y yield change · NASDAQ vs S&P 500

Rule applied: Risk-Off (scores × 0.65) needs two risk signals: VIX above 30, 10Y yield up more than 100 bps over 20 sessions, NASDAQ more than 5 percentage points behind the S&P 500 over its last 20 closes. Neutral (× 0.85) needs one risk signal or two neutral ones: VIX above 20, yield up more than 50 bps, NASDAQ more than 2 percentage points behind. Otherwise Risk-On (× 1.00). A missing signal doesn't vote.

About this dashboard

A daily signal dashboard tracking the AI supply chain — from power plants to software — identifying where investment opportunities and risks are building.

🔴 Hot / Bottleneck
Strongest bottleneck signal: layer score above 65. Colours hold until the score moves 10 points past the threshold.
🟠 Emerging
Building momentum toward a bottleneck: layer score 45–65.
🟢 Neutral / Healthy
Layer score 25–45. Growing, no strong bottleneck signal.
🔵 Cooling
Layer score below 25, or year-over-year revenue growth down more than 5 points at every company in the layer.
Warning flags

⚡ Narrative ahead of fundamentals — The layer's top-scoring company is in the news, but its revenue growth is slowing or barely accelerating.

hype badge on a company — Price up more than 15% in 30 days while revenue grows under 10%, or more than 20 points ahead of its layer peers while revenue growth slows, or up 15% or more and at least 10 points ahead of the NASDAQ while year-over-year revenue growth fell more than 5 points. Lowers that company's rating to C. It doesn't change the layer's colour.

revenue up, price down badge on a company — Revenue YoY accelerated more than 5 percentage points last quarter while the price fell 15% or more in 30 days and at least 10 points more than the NASDAQ. Two readings: the market may be pricing a risk that reported revenue doesn't show yet, or the price may have fallen further than the revenue trend implies. Not a recommendation. It changes no score, colour or rating.

What the colors mean
Red — current bottleneck
Layer score above 65 (market-cap weighted), held above 55 on 2 of the last 3 sessions. A score above 80 turns Red at once. The strongest bottleneck signal in the chain right now.
Orange — emerging
Layer score 45–65: building momentum toward a bottleneck.
Green — neutral / healthy
Layer score 25–45: growing, no strong bottleneck signal.
Blue — cooling
Layer score below 25, or year-over-year revenue growth down more than 5 points at every company in the layer.
Colours are held: a layer keeps its colour from recent sessions until its score moves more than 10 points past that colour's threshold (Red holds down to 55, Orange down to 35), so a layer can show a colour outside its score band.
Note on AI Security layer: Security companies (CRWD, PANW, S, OKTA) are steady compounders, not hardware bottlenecks. A Blue/Green Security layer is normal and healthy. Watch for Orange/Red only during major breach cycles or regulatory shifts.
